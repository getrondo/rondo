# Glossary

| Term | Ukrainian | Russian | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game | Гра | Игра | A scheduled session at a venue (may contain many mini-games). |
| Mini-game | Міні-гра / раунд | Мини-игра / круг | One match between two teams inside a session (e.g. 7 min or 2 goals). |
| Signup | Запис / «+» | Запись / «+» | A player's answer for a game: in, maybe or out. |
| Waitlist / reserve | Резерв | Резерв | Players beyond the cap, in order. |
| Cap | Ліміт | Лимит / «не более N чел» | Max players for the booked fields. |
| Late cancellation | Пізнє скасування | Поздняя отмена | Cancelling after the cutoff (e.g. 14:00 on game day). |
| No-show | Неявка | Неявка | Signed up, didn't come, didn't cancel. |
| Line-up | Склади | Составы | Players per team for the day. |
| Bibs | Маніжки | Манишки | Coloured training vests that identify teams. |
| Goalkeeper (GK) | Воротар | Вратарь | A scarce role with its own slots and price. |
| Guest | Гість («+1») | Гость («+1») | A player without Telegram, added by a sponsor. |
| Collector | Збирач коштів | Инкассатор | Helper who collects cash at the field. |
| Venue / field | Майданчик / поле | Площадка / поле | A sports centre and its numbered pitches. |
| Changing room | Роздягальня | Раздевалка | Numbered, key from reception or clubhouse. |
| Format | Формат | Формат | Teams × players × fields, e.g. 3×6 on one field. |
| Rotation | Ротація | Ротация | Rule for who plays next (winner stays, max 2 wins…). |
| Squad | Склад команди | Состав команды | Competitive (league) team subset of players. |
