# Vision

## Problem

An amateur football community in Vienna (~200 Telegram members, 15–40 players per game, 2–3 games a
week, plus competitive league teams) depends on **one organizer** who does everything by hand:

- posts and re-posts a "+ / −" poll for every game, with venue, time and capacity in the title;
- keeps the reserve list in his head and pings people one by one when a spot frees up;
- adds guests who aren't on Telegram to the count manually;
- decides on 1, 2 or 3 fields and confirms them with the venue before a deadline;
- watches the weather and cancels or reschedules;
- writes the team line-ups by hand and balances them from memory;
- collects cash, chases bank transfers and remembers who still owes;
- answers "which field? which changing room? who has the key?" before almost every game;
- tracks bibs, balls and lost items, writes birthday posts, moderates ads, and runs league logistics.

The organizer wrote **29% of all messages** in the group. When he is away or his poll breaks, the
community stalls. Players miss polls because they mute the noisy chat, go to the wrong venue after
late changes, and quietly remove their "+" without anyone noticing.

## Vision

> **Rondo turns one overloaded organizer into a well-run club.**

Every game has one live sign-up card that shows who's in, who's waiting, the venue pin, field,
changing room, price and who has paid. The waitlist promotes itself. Weather and capacity
decisions are proposed on time. Teams come out balanced from real history. After the whistle,
players rate the game in two taps. Over time the community gets real stats, fair teams and
recognition, and the organizer gets his evenings back.

## Users

| Persona | Needs |
|---|---|
| **Organizer** (1–2 people) | Create games fast, fill them, decide go/no-go, build fair teams, know who paid, delegate. |
| **Co-organizer / helper** | Collect cash, open the changing room, bring bibs and balls, stand in when the organizer is away. |
| **Regular player** (~25–60 people) | Sign up in one tap, know where and when, see their team, see their stats. |
| **Occasional player** (~500 aliases seen once to four times) | Easy to join, no group noise, clear rules. |
| **Guest** (no Telegram) | Be counted and assigned to a team via a member ("+1"). |
| **Goalkeeper** | A scarce role with its own slots and price. |
| **League squad member** | Fixture schedule, availability, results, travel. |

## Principles

1. **Replace the poll, keep the habit.** One-tap "I'm in / I'm out" in the group, live-updating. No commands to learn.
2. **Bot proposes, human decides.** Cancellations, team line-ups and fines are always confirmed by an organizer.
3. **Less noise in the group.** Logistics answers go to DMs or into the pinned game card; the group stays social.
4. **Language per person.** Ukrainian or Russian based on Telegram `language_code`, falling back to Ukrainian; English and German later.
5. **Privacy first.** Ratings hidden from everyone but admins; minimal personal data; export and delete on request (GDPR).
6. **Track money, don't move it.** A ledger of who owes and who paid, cash or SEPA, with no payment processing.
7. **Multi-activity ready.** Football first, but the same engine runs basketball, volleyball, table tennis and league squads.

## Success metrics (6 months after launch)

| Metric | Today (estimated from history) | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Organizer messages per game | ~25 | < 5 |
| "Where / which room / is there a spot?" questions per game | ~1–3 in chat | ~0 (answered by bot) |
| Late cancellations (after cutoff) per game | unknown, frequent | measured, −50% |
| Games cancelled for too few players announced on game day | several per season | 0, decided by deadline |
| Unpaid player-games at end of week | chased manually | < 2%, visible in ledger |
| Team balance complaints | recurring | rare; goal difference per mini-game trending down |
| Weekly active players | ~20–40 | +25% (more occasional players return) |
