# E12 · Recognition & community life

**Milestone:** M3 Community · **Priority:** P2

## Goal

Keep the social glue that makes this group special, with less manual work: birthdays, awards, hall of fame, season recap, events with cost splitting.

## Evidence from the community

- 174 birthday messages, mostly written by hand by the organizer.
- Awards ceremonies, 'best goalkeeper', a hall of fame (lifetime free play), merch.
- BBQs and watch parties with headcounts and receipt splitting.

## User stories

- **E12-S01** As a player, I can (optionally) store my birthday; the bot posts a greeting and the organizer can add a personal line.
- **E12-S02** As an organizer, I create season awards (most games, MVP count, iron man, best newcomer) computed from stats, with a community vote.
- **E12-S03** As an organizer, I maintain a hall of fame with perks (e.g. free play wired into E07 pricing).
- **E12-S04** As an organizer, I create a social event (BBQ, watch party) with RSVP, +1s, kids, and a receipt split (E07).
- **E12-S05** As a member, I get a 'Season Wrapped' recap card with my year in numbers.

## Acceptance criteria

- Birthday storage is opt-in; the year is optional.
- All recognition posts can be previewed and edited by the organizer before sending.

## Out of scope

- Merch shop
