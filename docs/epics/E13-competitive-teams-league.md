# E13 · Competitive teams, league & tournaments

**Milestone:** M3 Community · **Priority:** P2

## Goal

Support the community's league squads (main, second, veterans): fixtures, squad availability, call-ups, results and supporter turnout, separate from casual games.

## Evidence from the community

- The community fields several teams in an ÖKFB small-field league plus cups, indoor tournaments and a 35+ team.
- League results, scorers and protocol disputes are reconstructed from memory and video; fans ask 'what's the score?' during matches.
- Wednesday training slots are reserved for competition players.

## User stories

- **E13-S01** As a team manager, I create a squad (subset of players) and import or enter the season fixtures (opponent, venue, kick-off).
- **E13-S02** As a squad member, I mark availability per fixture; the manager sees who's available and calls up the matchday squad.
- **E13-S03** As a manager, I enter the final score, scorers and cards; the league standings view updates.
- **E13-S04** As a fan, I get a 'come support us' post with time and venue, and live score updates from a volunteer.
- **E13-S05** As a manager, I run squad-only training sessions using the normal game flow (E03) with restricted signup.

## Acceptance criteria

- Squads are scoped: casual players don't get squad notifications.
- If ÖKFB provides a feed or API, fixtures can be synced (spike first).

## Out of scope

- Official league registration paperwork
